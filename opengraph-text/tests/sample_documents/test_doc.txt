Anthropic, an AI safety company based in San Francisco, announced today that
its CEO Dario Amodei will keynote an upcoming conference on responsible AI
development. The company, founded in 2021, has raised significant funding
from investors including Google and Spark Capital.

Anthropic's flagship product, Claude, is a large language model used by
developers and enterprises to build AI-powered applications. The company
has stated that its mission is to ensure that transformative AI is developed
safely and benefits all of humanity.

In a related development, OpenAI, a competitor of Anthropic, also based in
San Francisco, released a new version of its GPT model this week. Industry
analysts see the two companies as leading a broader race toward advanced
AI systems.
