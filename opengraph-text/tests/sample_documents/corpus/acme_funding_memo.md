# Acme Corp Series A Memo

Acme Corp, a fintech startup based in San Francisco, has raised a $10M Series A
led by Northbridge Ventures. The round values the company at $60M post-money.

Jane Doe, Acme Corp's co-founder and CEO, previously worked as an engineering
lead at Globex Inc before starting the company in 2021. She holds a computer
science degree and has spoken publicly about the challenges of building
compliant payment infrastructure for small businesses.

Acme Corp's flagship product is a payments API that lets merchants accept
card and bank transfers without managing PCI compliance themselves. The
company says it processed over $200M in transaction volume last year, up
from $40M the year before.

Northbridge Ventures partner Marcus Lin will join Acme Corp's board as part
of the deal. Lin previously led Northbridge's investment in Globex Inc, and
says he has been tracking the payments infrastructure space closely.

Acme Corp plans to use the new funding to expand its engineering team and
launch a fraud-detection product later this year. The company is a direct
competitor to Fintaro, another payments startup that raised a larger round
last quarter.
