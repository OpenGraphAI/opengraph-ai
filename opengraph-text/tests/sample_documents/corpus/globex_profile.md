# Company Profile: Globex Inc

Globex Inc is a mid-sized enterprise software company headquartered in
Austin, Texas. Founded in 2009, the company builds infrastructure tooling
for large financial institutions and has grown to roughly 800 employees.

Globex Inc has historically served as a training ground for fintech
founders; several alumni have gone on to start their own companies,
including Jane Doe, who worked as an engineering lead there before founding
Acme Corp in 2021.

The company's CEO, Robert Chen, has led Globex Inc since 2015 and previously
held a senior role at a large bank. Under his leadership, Globex Inc
acquired two smaller data-analytics firms and expanded into cloud-based
compliance tooling.

Globex Inc is not currently seeking outside investment, having been
profitable since 2018. It remains privately held and has no plans for a
public offering in the near term.
