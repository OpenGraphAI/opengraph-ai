# The Rise of Anthropic

Anthropic, an AI safety company founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei and
Daniela Amodei, has become one of the most prominent research labs
working on large language models. Headquartered in San Francisco,
the company received significant funding from Google and later from
Amazon. Its flagship product, Claude, competes directly with OpenAI's
ChatGPT in the enterprise AI market.

Dario Amodei previously worked at OpenAI as VP of Research before
co-founding Anthropic with several colleagues. The company has focused
heavily on Constitutional AI as a training methodology.
